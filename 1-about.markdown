<p>Hi there! I'm Sanket, a finance professional, adventure seeker, and a product of life's ups and downs. Born in India, my journey has taken me across the globe, living in the US, Singapore, and now Canada for the last 10 years. These rich cultural experiences have shaped me into an open-minded individual, always curious and always eager to learn and grow.</p>

<p>My professional journey is a testament to my adaptability and strong work ethic. From scrubbing dishes at a yacht club during university to leading multiple $100M investments and shaping corporate strategy, I've never shied away from hard work. My humble beginnings have instilled in me a deep appreciation for every opportunity that has come my way.</p>

<p>In the corporate world, I've had the privilege of working with renowned organizations like <strong>Cadillac Fairview</strong>, <strong>British Columbia Investments</strong>, and <strong>BNP Paribas</strong>. I've led ~$450M in investments and shaped the future of a $35B real estate fund. My expertise lies in investment research, strategy, and business transformation. I thrive on tackling complex challenges and driving meaningful results.</p>

<p>But my experience extends outside large corporate settings. Running <strong>Hotel Royal Embassy</strong>, a small business, gave me a taste of entrepreneurship and the challenges that come with it. With minimal resources, I learned to be resourceful and decisive, bringing a sense of agency to difficult problems. Although brief, this stint fundamentally changed my approach to work. No matter the context, I bring an ownership mentality to the table, not an employee mentality. This is where my work ethic, creativity, and optimism stem from in all that I do.</p>

<p>Beyond the boardroom, I'm an adrenaline junkie at heart, always seeking new adventures. From scuba diving in exotic locations to trekking the Himalayas, I find joy in pushing my limits and immersing myself in nature's wonders. When I'm not chasing thrills, you can find me on the badminton court or experimenting with new recipes in the kitchen.</p>

<p>I'm also deeply passionate about financial education. Through my upcoming website, <strong>SayEBITDAH.com</strong>, I aim to demystify complex financial concepts and empower curious people like me. While the website comes live, you can find a lot of my essays <a href="http://www.sanketshreemal.com/notes/">here</a> on this website.</p>

<p>Inspired by the likes of Napoleon, Genghis Khan, and Mozart, I believe in the power of ambition, determination, and creativity. I'm on a mission to leave my mark on the world, one adventure and achievement at a time.</p>

<p>My life so far has been a colorful canvas of experiences that have built a character and personality I am proud of. Can't wait to see what's next! </p>
